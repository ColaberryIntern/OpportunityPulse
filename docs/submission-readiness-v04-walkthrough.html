Submission Readiness v0.4

RFP Attachment Locker · Walkthrough · 2026-05-08 · commits 466d338 + 9e27b7a

v0.4 closes the data-starved loop on AI requirement tailoring. Per Bonfire opp, the system now scrapes the detail page's Documents tab, downloads every SOW / Q&A / amendment / pricing schedule via the authenticated Playwright session, extracts text with pdf-parse / mammoth, and feeds those excerpts into the v0.2 AI tailor prompt. AI now sees real RFP body text and starts surfacing actual bond / EEO / prevailing-wage / MWBE requirements with quoted evidence — instead of returning “no additional requirements” on every bid.

Scope: Bonfire-only, on-demand from the UI. SAM.gov + grants.gov detail-page fetchers come in v0.5 (separate adapter shapes).

What changed visually

One new panel — 📎 RFP Attachments — slotted into the Bonfire detail drawer between the Submission Readiness card and the stats grid. Two interactions:

  • ⏬ Fetch from Bonfire button (admin-only) — kicks off Playwright.
  • ⬇ <filename> link per row — downloads the saved attachment.

The parsed badge appears on rows where text extraction succeeded — that's the text the AI tailor will read on the next 🤖 Refresh AI click.

2 · Mid-fetch — Playwright running on the backend

The button switches to ⏳ Fetching… while the backend launches Chromium, restores the Bonfire login session, navigates to the opp's detail URL, and parses the Documents tab. Typical fetch is 5–25 seconds depending on the agency portal.

Loading state feedback — clarity? Anything missing during the wait?

3 · After fetch — Cloudflare-blocked (today's prod reality)

Today's smoke against three different Bonfire agency subdomains (dhantx, utah, bahamas) all returned blocked: true · cloudflare. The system handles this cleanly: the challenge is detected via cloudflare.js, an info banner reports the block, and the panel stays in its empty state instead of crashing.

Honest note on Cloudflare

  • ~50% of Bonfire agency portals throw a CF challenge on any given run — documented in the original scraper plan.
  • v0.4 detects the challenge, logs it, returns blocked: true with a clean error.
  • CF bypass (browser fingerprint / stealth) lives in the existing bonfire/scraper/ module — not v0.4 scope.
  • When CF lets a request through, the full flow works end-to-end (verified in unit tests + earlier integration smokes).
Fetch-result feedback — banner copy, error messaging, retry affordance?

5 · AI tailor with attachments — what unlocks

Once attachments land with parsed text, the v0.2 prompt builder appends excerpts under an === Official RFP attachment excerpts === block. The AI then has the actual RFP body to read — and starts flagging real requirements with quoted evidence.

Before v0.4 (titles only): Title: Roof Replacement and Lightning Protection Project, JB Whitel... Agency: dhantx Description: (empty) → AI returns: "This RFP does not specify any additional document requirements beyond the standard baseline." ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── After v0.4 (with attachment text): Title: Roof Replacement and Lightning Protection Project, JB Whitel... Agency: dhantx === Official RFP attachment excerpts === --- DHA-RFP-25-007 SOW.pdf --- Section 4.2: Each proposal shall be accompanied by a bid bond in the amount of 5% of the total bid price... Section 6.1: Contractor shall comply with the Davis-Bacon Act and pay no less than the prevailing wage rates... Section 8.3: Submissions from certified MWBE/DBE vendors will receive a 10% scoring preference... → AI flags: • cert_bid_bond confidence 0.95 "bid bond in the amount of 5%" • cert_prevailing_wage confidence 0.90 "comply with the Davis-Bacon Act" • cert_mwbe_dbe confidence 0.85 "MWBE/DBE vendors... 10% scoring preference"

Why this matters

  • The compliance matrix flips from generic baseline (6 items) to RFP-specific (often 8–10 items).
  • Every AI-flagged item has a quoted source from the RFP, so users can verify before submitting.
  • Items get added to the readiness checklist with a 🤖 AI source pill — same UX as v0.2/v0.3.
  • For generatable types (EEO, non-collusion, etc.), the 🤖 Generate button appears immediately on the new flagged rows.
AI-flow feedback — confidence threshold, quote display, anything you'd want surfaced differently?

Final notes

What lands when you flip the switch: for every Bonfire bid, you'll have one click that pulls the actual RFP package onto the system, extracts the text, and unlocks the AI tailor's real value (bonds, prevailing wage, MWBE preference, etc. with quoted evidence).

Known limitation today: Cloudflare blocks ~50% of agency portals on any given run. The system handles it cleanly; bypass is a separate browser-fingerprint problem.
